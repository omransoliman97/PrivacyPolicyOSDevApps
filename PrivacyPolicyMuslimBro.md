## 1. Eligibility and Acceptance

The App is intended for general audiences. If you are a minor in your jurisdiction, you may only use the App with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian. ## 2. Summary

- The App does **not** require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or other account credentials on a server.
- Your prayer-time settings, tracker history, favorite mosques, and other preferences are stored **locally on your device**.
- The App uses your **location** (with your permission) to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction, and nearby mosques.
- The App uses **Google AdMob** to show ads, and separate third-party APIs (described below) to calculate prayer times and look up nearby mosques — each may collect certain data as described in Sections 4 and 5.
- You can export, import, or delete your locally stored data at any time from within the App, or remove all data by uninstalling the App.
- The App, its content, and the information it displays (prayer times, Hijri dates, Qibla direction, and mosque listings) are provided on an **"as is" and "as available" basis, without warranty of any kind** — see Section 8.

## 3. Information We Collect

### a) Location Data
If you grant permission, the App accesses your device's GPS/network location (or a city you search for manually) in order to:
- Calculate daily prayer times for your area.
- Determine the Qibla direction from your position.
- Determine your Hijri calendar date and local holidays.
- Find mosques near you, when you use the Nearby Mosques feature.

Your coordinates are sent to the third-party APIs described in Section 4 to retrieve this information. Location data is cached locally on your device so the App can work offline. We do not use your location for any purpose other than the features described above, and we do not sell your location to data brokers.

You may deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings; you can still use most of the App by manually searching for and selecting a city, though the Nearby Mosques feature specifically requires a location to function.

### b) App Preferences, Prayer Tracker, and Favorites
The App stores, only on your device:
- Your display name (if you choose to enter one), used only to personalize greetings shown to you within the App.
- Your chosen prayer calculation method, time format, theme, language, and notification preferences.
- Your prayer-tracker history (which prayers you've marked as completed and when).
- Your Nearby Mosques search settings (radius, result limit) and any mosques you save as favorites.

This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party, except as part of a backup file that you explicitly choose to export and share yourself (see Section 6), or the minimal data described in Section 3(a)/Section 4 sent to third-party APIs to retrieve prayer times or mosque listings.

### c) Advertising and Device Identifiers
The App displays banner advertisements served through **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device type and operating system, and general app usage/interaction data related to ads. This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy and advertising policies, linked in Section 4. We do not control, and are not given access to, the individual advertising identifiers Google collects.

### d) Information We Do Not Collect
We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying account information on our servers, because the App has no account system or backend server for personal data. We do not access your contacts, photos, messages, or other apps.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use the information described above solely to:
- Calculate and display prayer times, Qibla direction, Hijri calendar information, and nearby mosques for your location.
- Remember your preferences, prayer-tracking history, and favorites between app sessions.
- Send you local notifications/reminders for prayer times, if you enable them (scheduled entirely on your device, requiring no data to be sent anywhere).
- Display advertisements that help keep the App free to use.
- Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the App.
- Comply with applicable law or respond to lawful requests from public authorities.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not sell your personal information for money. To the extent any data sharing described above could be considered a "sale" or "share" under laws like the CCPA, you can limit it by declining location and advertising permissions in your device settings.

## 6. Your Data, Your Control

- **Local storage only.** With the exception of the minimal data sent to AlAdhan.com and the Mosque Finder API (your coordinates, to calculate prayer times or find mosques) and to Google AdMob (as described above), your settings, tracker history, and favorites never leave your device.
- **Export/Import.** The Settings screen lets you export your settings and tracker data to a file you control, and import a previously exported file. You decide if, how, and with whom that file is shared.
- **Deletion.** You can clear the App's data at any time via your device's App Info settings, or simply by uninstalling the App, which permanently removes all locally stored data.
- **Notification permissions.** You can disable prayer notifications at any time in Settings or in your device's system settings.
- **Location permissions.** You can revoke location access at any time in your device's system settings without losing the ability to use most of the App (you can instead search for your city manually), though Nearby Mosques specifically requires a location.
