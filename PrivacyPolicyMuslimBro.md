## 2. Summary

- The App does **not** require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, or other account credentials on a server.
- Your prayer-time settings, tracker history, favorite mosques, favorited Quran verses, Zakat Calculator entries, and other preferences are stored **locally on your device**.
- The App uses your **location** (with your permission) to calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction, and nearby mosques. It reads your location only while the App is open — never in the background.
- The App uses **Google AdMob** to show ads — including optional rewarded video ads you choose to watch in exchange for extended access to the home screen widget — and separate third-party services (described below) to calculate prayer times, search for cities, name locations, look up nearby mosques, retrieve Quran text/audio, and fetch current gold/silver prices for the Zakat Calculator; each may collect certain data as described in Sections 3 and 4. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for your ad-consent choices first.
- The App has **no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own** and no push-notification server; prayer reminders are scheduled on your device.
- You can export and import your settings and tracker history from within the App, and erase everything the App has stored on your device with **Delete All Data** (or by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App) — see Section 6.
- The App, its content, and the information it displays (prayer times, Hijri dates, Qibla direction, Quran text/translations, and mosque listings) are provided on an **"as is" and "as available" basis, without warranty of any kind** — see Section 8.

## 3. Information We Collect

### a) Location Data
If you grant permission, the App accesses your device's GPS/network location (or uses a city you search for manually) in order to:
- Calculate daily prayer times for your area.
- Show the Qibla direction from your position (calculated on your device).
- Determine your Hijri calendar date and local holidays.
- Find mosques near you, when you use the Nearby Mosques feature (which asks your device for a fresh, higher-accuracy position and, on iOS, may ask for temporary full-accuracy access).
- Update your saved location automatically when you've traveled a significant distance, if you turn on **Auto-update location when I travel** in Settings.

The App reads your location only while it is open and in use — it does not request background location access. (The periodic widget refresh described in Section 3(f) uses only your previously saved coordinates.) Your coordinates — or the city you chose — are sent to the third-party services described in Section 4 only as needed to retrieve prayer times and Hijri dates, to look up nearby mosques, and to turn coordinates into place names. Your saved location is cached locally on your device so the App can work offline. We do not use your location for any purpose other than the features described above, and we do not sell your location to data brokers.

You may deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings; you can still use most of the App by manually searching for and selecting a city, though the Nearby Mosques feature specifically requires a location to function.

### b) App Preferences, Prayer Tracker, and Favorites
The App stores, only on your device:
- Your display name (if you choose to enter one), used only to personalize greetings shown to you within the App.
- Your saved location (coordinates, place name, and country) and whether it was detected automatically or chosen manually.
- Your chosen prayer calculation method (including any custom-method settings), Asr setting, time format, theme, language, notification choices (including your chosen Adhan voice), and any minute adjustments you make to individual prayer times.
- Your prayer-tracker history (which prayers you've marked as completed and when).
- Your Nearby Mosques search radius and any mosques you save as favorites.
- Your Quran reading and listening preferences (display mode, translation, reciter, audio quality, playback speed), downloaded translations and recitation audio, and any ayahs you favorite.
- Your Zakat Calculator entries (cash, bank balances, gold and silver quantities, business assets, investments, money owed to you, debts, and your chosen currency and nisab standard) and the most recently fetched metal prices. These are financial figures you type in; they are used only to compute your result on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party (only your chosen currency code is sent to the price service described in Section 4).
- Your favorite Explore topics.
- The home screen widget's current access status (i.e., when its free or ad-earned access expires) and the prayer times and labels it displays — stored locally so the widget itself can show them and check access without needing the App to be open; never transmitted anywhere.

This information is never transmitted to us or to any third party, except as part of a backup file that you explicitly choose to export and share yourself (see Section 6), or the minimal data described in Sections 3(a) and 4 sent to third-party services to retrieve prayer times, find cities and mosques, name locations, or fetch Quran content and Zakat metal prices.

### c) Advertising and Device Identifiers
The App displays advertisements served through **Google AdMob** — banner ads shown passively within the App, and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to start (via a confirmation prompt) in exchange for extending the home screen widget's access window. To serve and measure these ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device type and operating system, and general app usage/interaction data related to ads. On Android, Google's ads SDK may also use Android's advertising ID and, where your device supports them, Privacy Sandbox ad-related services; you can manage or reset these in your device's ads/privacy settings. This information is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy and advertising policies, linked in Section 4. We do not control, and are not given access to, the individual advertising identifiers Google collects. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional — the widget also includes a free access period on first install that requires no ad at all.

**Ad consent.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where the law requires it, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your choice about personalized ads before any ad is requested; your choice determines whether the ads you see can be personalized. Where that consent flow applies to you, an **Ad Privacy Choices** entry appears under Settings → About so you can review or change your choice at any time.

### d) Information We Do Not Collect
We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying account information on our servers, because the App has no account system or backend server for personal data. The App does not include its own analytics or crash-reporting tools. We do not access your contacts, photos, messages, camera, microphone, or other apps.

### e) Device Permissions and Sensors
The App requests the following device permissions, each only for the purpose described:
- **Location (precise and approximate)** — prayer times, Qibla, and Nearby Mosques (see Section 3(a)). Used in the foreground only.
- **Notifications** — to show prayer reminders and the Quran playback controls.
- **Exact alarms (Android)** — so prayer reminders and the Adhan sound at the right minute.
- **Internet and network state** — to retrieve the data described in Section 4 and to download content you ask for.
- **Foreground service for media playback, and wake lock (Android)** — to keep Quran recitation playing, with its notification and lock-screen controls, while the App is in the background or the screen is off.
- **Battery-optimization exemption request (Android)** — the App asks once, through the system's own dialog, to be excluded from battery optimization, because some phones otherwise stop Quran playback and widget updates in the background. Declining has no effect on your data.
- **Run at device start-up (Android)** — so scheduled prayer reminders and the widget's refresh schedule can resume after your device restarts.
- **Advertising ID** — added by Google's ads SDK (see Section 3(c)).

The Qibla compass reads your device's motion and magnetic-field sensors locally to show direction; those readings are not stored or transmitted.

### f) Background Activity
Some features keep working while the App is closed or in the background:
- **Prayer reminders and the Adhan** are scheduled on your device and delivered by the system's alarm and notification services; no server is involved.
- **Quran playback** can continue in the background, with a media notification, until you stop it.
- **Home screen widget refresh (Android).** The App schedules a periodic background task, whether or not you have added the widget, that recalculates the widget's prayer times from your saved location even when the App isn't open. This normally uses data already cached on your device; when fresh data is needed, it contacts AlAdhan.com with your saved coordinates and calculation settings, exactly as the App does when it is open. The widget also updates its own countdown locally, without using the network. (On iOS, the widget is refreshed by the system's own widget scheduling.)

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for the privacy or data practices of these third parties, and your use of features backed by them is also subject to their own terms:

| Service | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| **AlAdhan.com API** (api.aladhan.com) | Calculates daily and monthly prayer times and Hijri calendar dates | Latitude/longitude, the calculation method and Asr setting you've selected, and — if you use a custom method — its parameters |
| **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) | Looks up cities when you search for a location manually | The city or place name you type |
| **Your device's geocoder** (provided by the operating system — on Android typically through Google Play services; on iOS, Apple) | Turns coordinates into a place name or address (for example, the city label for your location, or an address for a mosque that has none in the map data) | Coordinates — yours, or those of a mosque |
| **OpenStreetMap Overpass API** (public servers overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, and overpass.private.coffee, tried in that order) | Looks up mosques near a location for the Nearby Mosques feature | Latitude/longitude and your chosen search radius |
| **AlQuran Cloud API** (alquran.cloud) | Provides Quran text and translations, surah/edition metadata, and the list of available reciters | The edition, surah, or ayah you request — no personal or location data |
| **Islamic Network CDN** (cdn.islamic.network) | Delivers Quran audio recitation files | The reciter, ayah, and audio quality you request — no personal or location data |
| **xaus.com API** | Provides current gold/silver spot prices for the Zakat Calculator | Your selected display currency only — no personal or location data |
| **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and **Google's User Messaging Platform** | Displays in-app banner ads and optional rewarded video ads, and collects ad-consent choices where required | Advertising identifier, IP address, device/app data, and your consent choices, as described in Section 3(c) |
| **Third-party map apps** (e.g. Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze) | Opened only when you tap "Open" on a mosque to get directions | The mosque's name, address, and coordinates, passed to whichever app you choose — this only happens if you tap that button |
| **Google Play Store and the system share sheet** | Opened only when you tap a link to another app (for example "Our Other Apps" or the Tasbih promotion on the Home screen) or choose to share an ayah, a Name of Allah, a mosque, or an app link | Whatever you choose to open or share, which the destination app or store receives under its own policies |

## 6. Your Data, Your Control

- **Local storage only.** With the exception of the minimal data described in Section 4 (sent to AlAdhan.com, OpenStreetMap's Nominatim and Overpass services, your device's geocoder, AlQuran Cloud, the Islamic Network CDN, and xaus.com to retrieve prayer times, find cities and mosques, name locations, and fetch Quran content and Zakat metal prices, respectively) and to Google AdMob (as described above), your settings, tracker history, favorites, and Zakat entries never leave your device.
- **Export/Import.** The Settings screen lets you export your settings and tracker data to a file you control, and import a previously exported file (which replaces your current settings and tracker data). The backup file contains your display name, saved location, calculation and notification settings, and tracker history; it does not include favorites, Zakat entries, or downloaded Quran content. You decide if, how, and with whom that file is shared, so keep it somewhere private.
- **Deletion.** Settings → Data → **Delete All Data** permanently erases everything the App has stored on your device: your settings (including your name and saved location), prayer-tracker history, cached prayer times, favorites (ayahs, mosques, and Explore topics), Zakat Calculator entries, mosque search settings, Quran preferences, downloaded translations and recitation audio, and the data the home screen widget stores. It also cancels your scheduled prayer reminders and returns the App to first-time setup. Two things are outside the App's reach, so it can't erase them: backup files you exported, which remain wherever you saved them until you delete them, and your ad-consent choice, which Google's consent SDK stores itself (see **Ad choices** below). Clearing the App's storage in your device settings (on Android, typically Settings → Apps → Muslim Bro → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data, including that consent record.
- **Notification permissions.** You can set any prayer to silent from the App's Prayers screen, or disable notifications for the App at any time in your device's system settings.
- **Location permissions.** You can revoke location access at any time in your device's system settings without losing the ability to use most of the App (you can instead search for your city manually), though Nearby Mosques specifically requires a location.
- **Ad choices.** Where the consent flow described in Section 3(c) applies to you, you can change your choice at any time under Settings → About → Ad Privacy Choices. On any device you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings.

## 7. Disclaimer of Accuracy

Prayer times, Qibla direction, Hijri calendar dates, mosque listings, Quran text/translations, Zakat calculations, and estimated walking/driving times displayed in the App are calculated or retrieved automatically from third-party sources and **are provided for general convenience only**. They may not be perfectly accurate, may not reflect the specific rulings of your local mosque or religious authority, and may be affected by calculation-method differences, data errors, GPS/location inaccuracy, or third-party service downtime.

**You are solely responsible for verifying prayer times and any other religiously significant information independently**, including with your local mosque, Islamic center, or religious authority, before relying on it for religious observance. We make no representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of any information the App displays, and we are not liable for any consequence — religious, personal, or otherwise — arising from reliance on it.

**Reminders and the Adhan.** Prayer reminders and Adhan sounds are delivered by your device's alarm and notification systems. They can be delayed, silenced, or blocked by device settings — for example battery optimization, Do Not Disturb or silent mode, denied notification or exact-alarm permissions, or phone-maker power-saving features — so please don't rely on them as your only way of knowing prayer times.

Similarly, mosque listings (including name, address, and distance) come from a third-party directory we do not operate or verify, and walking/driving time estimates are rough approximations calculated from straight-line distance, not an actual route — they are explicitly labeled as estimates within the App and should not be relied upon for precise timing or navigation-critical decisions.

The **Zakat Calculator** is a general-guidance tool only: it does not account for every situation (retirement accounts, agricultural produce, livestock, and other cases with their own separate rules), the metal prices it uses are indicative market rates from a third-party API rather than a settlement-grade or transactional quote, and which nisab standard or scholarly position applies to your own circumstances is a religious determination this App does not make for you. **Consult a qualified scholar before relying on any figure the Zakat Calculator produces**, particularly given the real financial and religious obligations involved.
