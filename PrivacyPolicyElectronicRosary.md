# Privacy Policy for Tasbih - Rosary

**Last updated: [31/07/2026]**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tasbih ("the App," "we," "us," or
"our") handles information when you use it. Tasbih was built to work
with as little data collection as possible: your prayers, dhikr counts,
and progress are stored locally on your device, not on our servers, and
the App does not require you to create an account to use it.

This Policy applies to the Tasbih mobile application on Android and
iOS. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If
you do not agree, please do not use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Tasbih does not require registration, sign-in, or any personal
information (name, email, phone number) to function. Any prayers,
dhikr names, counts, targets, and settings you create exist only within
the App and are stored locally on your device.

If you choose to export a backup of your data, that file is created
and saved wherever you choose (your device's storage, or a cloud
service you separately control) — we never receive a copy of it.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Like most apps, Tasbih relies on third-party software development kits
(SDKs) that may automatically collect certain technical information as
part of how they function:

- **Advertising identifiers and device information**, collected by
  Google AdMob when the App shows a rewarded video ad (used in one
  place: to optionally extend the home-screen widget's active period).
  This can include your advertising ID, device and app information,
  general (non-precise) location derived from your IP address, and ad
  interaction data.
- **Basic network request data**, when the App fetches fonts from
  Google Fonts to render its typography. This can expose your IP
  address to Google as part of a standard font-loading request.

We do not operate our own analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking
services, and we do not otherwise collect usage data ourselves. Every
item above comes from the third-party services described in Section 4.

### 2.3 Permissions

Depending on your device settings, the App may request permission to
show notifications, for optional daily reminders. You can deny or
revoke this at any time in your device's settings; doing so only
disables reminders and does not affect any other part of the App.

## 3. How We Use Information

Because the App itself does not collect personal data beyond what's
described above, there is very little for us to "use." The technical
and advertising data described in Section 2.2 is used by our
third-party providers to:

- Serve and measure the rewarded ads that can extend the home-screen
  widget's active period
- Detect fraud and abuse of the ad-serving system
- Operate and improve their own respective services, under their own
  privacy policies (see Section 4)

We do not sell your data. We do not use data for purposes unrelated to
operating the App and the third-party services it relies on, and we do
not combine data across users to build individual profiles beyond
whatever our advertising provider does on its own platform.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Tasbih uses the following third-party services, each governed by its
own privacy policy:

- **Google AdMob** (advertising): [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- **Google Fonts** (typography): [Google Fonts Privacy Policy FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy)

We do not control these third parties' data practices and encourage
you to review their policies directly. Where your device or region
offers a way to limit ad personalization — such as resetting your
advertising ID, or your OS's "Ask App Not to Track" / limit ad tracking
setting — those controls apply to ads shown in this App as well.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade personal information with third
parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may be disclosed
only:

- To the third-party service providers listed in Section 4, strictly
  as needed for them to provide their service (e.g., serving an ad)
- If required by law, legal process, or governmental request
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or the
  public
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in
  which case this Policy would continue to apply to any transferred
  information until updated

## 6. Data Storage and Security

Your prayers, dhikr entries, counts, targets, and app preferences are
stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage. We
keep no server-side copy of this data, because we never receive it —
it never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot
guarantee absolute security of information stored on your device or
transmitted by third-party SDKs. You are responsible for securing your
own device (e.g., with a passcode or biometric lock).

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store your personal app data on any
server, there is nothing on our end to retain or delete. All in-app
data is removed automatically when you uninstall the App, or you can
clear it at any time using your device's app-storage settings.

For data collected by third-party SDKs (Section 2.2), retention is
governed by that provider's own policy — see the links in Section 4.

Tasbih has no user accounts, so there is no separate account-deletion
process to describe: uninstalling the App removes everything the App
itself stored.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Tasbih is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum
age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided us
with personal information, please contact us using the details in
Section 14 so we can address it.

Note for the developer: Google AdMob serves ads based on the audience
settings configured for this App in Google Play Console / App Store
Connect. Revisit AdMob's own guidance on child-directed treatment if
this App's declared audience ever changes.

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your information
under laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation
(GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), or similar
regional laws. Because Tasbih itself holds no personal data on any
server, most of these rights — access, correction, deletion,
portability — are already fully in your control on-device: through the
App's own export feature, your device's settings, or by uninstalling
the App.

For data handled by our third-party providers (Section 4), you can
exercise your rights directly with them — Google provides its own
tools for this, such as [Google Account data & privacy controls](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy)
— or contact us and we will assist where we're able to.

You can also limit ad personalization at any time through your
device's OS-level settings (for example, "Opt out of Ads
Personalization" on Android, or Limit Ad Tracking / App Tracking
Transparency on iOS).

## 10. International Users

around the world. Our third-party service providers (Section 4) may
process and store information on servers located outside your own
country, including in the United States. By using the App, you
understand that information may be transferred to and processed in
countries whose data protection laws may differ from those in your
own country.
