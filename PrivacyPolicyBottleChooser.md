# Privacy Policy for Bottle Chooser

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

## Overview
Bottle Chooser ("we", "us", "our"), published by OSDev, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Bottle Chooser collects, how it's used, and your choices — for both the app and this policy itself.

## Information We Collect
- **Device and advertising information**: when an ad is requested, our ad partner Google AdMob (through the Google Mobile Ads SDK) collects device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID / Apple's IDFA), device model, OS version, your IP address (from which it may infer an approximate location), and information about how ads are shown and interacted with. This information is processed by Google to deliver and measure ads. We only see aggregated reports in our AdMob account.
- **Consent choices**: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Bottle Chooser shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) the first time you open the app. Your choices are stored on your device and read by the ad SDK. You can review or change them at any time in Settings > Ad Consent.
- **Usage data**: we do not collect usage analytics or crash reports beyond what Google Play / the App Store may report to us in aggregate through their own developer consoles.
- **Location**: we do not collect location data. Bottle Chooser has no location-based features.
- **User content**: Bottle Chooser has no text entry, accounts, or saved lists — there's nothing for you to create or store. Your Settings choices (theme, accent color, language, whether the first-launch hint has been shown, and ad-consent status) are saved only on your own device, and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **Account information**: Bottle Chooser does not require an account and stores no personal account information.

## How We Use Information
Settings saved on your device are used only to remember your preferences. Information processed by Google AdMob is used solely to serve and measure ads, and to respect your consent choices. We do not use it for any other purpose.

## Third-Party Services
Bottle Chooser uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:
- **Google AdMob** (advertising) — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google User Messaging Platform** (consent management) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

For more on how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Data Sharing & Disclosure
We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the third-party services listed above, as required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Bottle Chooser, our users, or the public.

## Data Retention
Device identifiers used for advertising persist until you reset them at the OS level. Settings stored on your device remain until you uninstall the app or clear its data. We do not otherwise retain data, since we do not collect anything beyond what AdMob requires to serve ads.

## Your Rights & Choices
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your information, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of personalized advertising. You can:
- change your ad-personalization choice in the app at any time, in Settings > Ad Consent (where available in your region);
- reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad tracking, in your device settings (on Android, typically Settings > Google > Ads; on iOS, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and Apple Advertising);
- contact us at omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com.

Because we do not collect personal information ourselves, requests about data held by Google AdMob are best directed to Google through its own privacy tools.

## Children's Privacy
Bottle Chooser is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app may be used by a mixed-age audience (it's a group party game); we do not knowingly serve behavioral or interest-based advertising to users who identify as under 13. If we learn we've collected personal information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly. A parent or guardian who believes their child has provided us with personal information can contact us at omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com to request its deletion.

## Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

## International Data Transfers
Bottle Chooser is developed in France/EU. If you're located outside France/EU, your information may be transferred to and processed in France/EU or other countries where our service providers operate.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and encourage you to review this policy periodically.
