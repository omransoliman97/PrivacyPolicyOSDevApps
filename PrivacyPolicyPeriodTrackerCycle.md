# Privacy Policy — Period Tracker

**Last updated:** August 10, 2026
**Developer:** OSDev
**Contact:** [omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com]

This policy explains what information Period Tracker (the "app") collects,
how it's used, and what choices you have. It applies to the Period Tracker
app on Android and iOS, not to any third-party site or app you reach by
tapping a link from within it (such as our Play Store developer page).

## Summary

Your cycle data — the dates, notes, and anything else you log — is stored
only on your own device and is never sent to us or to anyone else, because
we don't operate a server for it. The only information that leaves your
device is standard advertising/technical data collected by our ad
provider, Google AdMob, and it is never linked to your cycle data or your
name. The sections below cover this in full detail, including what you can
do about it.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide

- **Your name** (optional, entered during setup or in Settings) — used only
  to personalize greetings inside the app.
- **Cycle data you log** — period start/end dates, flow intensity, and any
  notes you add.
- **Preferences** — reminder times, theme, and language.

All of the above is stored exclusively in a local database on your device.
We do not receive it, store it on any server, or transmit it anywhere —
with one exception: if you choose to use the app's Export feature, it
creates a backup file that you then share, save, or store wherever you
choose — at that point it's outside our hands and yours to manage.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically via advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads
(for example, to unlock extra home-screen-widget days). To serve, measure,
and (where you've allowed it) personalize these ads, Google AdMob and its
advertising partners may automatically collect:

- Device and advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID,
  or Apple's IDFA where you've granted tracking permission)
- General device and technical information (device model, operating
  system version, general/coarse location such as country or region,
  language)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, rewarded ads completed)

This information is collected and processed by Google, under Google's own
privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) — not by us. It is
never linked to your name, your cycle data, or anything else you type into
the app; our app has no way to connect the two even if it wanted to, since
the ad SDK and the local database are entirely separate. On iOS, this only
happens if you allow it via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; on
Android, you can reset or limit ad personalization in your device's Ads
settings at any time.

We do not use any analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or tracking SDK
beyond Google AdMob. We have no user accounts, no login, and no backend
server of our own.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To personalize the app for you (your name, your predictions, your
  reminders) — computed entirely on your device.
- To show ads, including rewarded ads where you opt in to one.
- We do not use your cycle data, notes, or name for advertising,
  profiling, marketing, or any purpose beyond making the app work for you.

## 3. Sharing and Sale of Information

**We do not sell your information**, and we never will. We do not share
your cycle data, name, or notes with anyone, for any reason — we
technically can't, because we never receive it in the first place. The
only information that leaves your device is (a) the advertising/technical
data described in §1.2, which goes to Google AdMob to serve ads, and (b)
your own manually-exported backup file, which goes only where you choose
to send it.

If we were ever required by law to disclose information, we have none of
your cycle data to disclose, since we never collect or hold it.

## 4. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

All app data lives in a local database on your device, protected by your
device's own operating-system-level storage protections. It stays there
until you remove it yourself — by deleting individual entries in the app,
using "Delete all data" in Settings, or uninstalling the app (which erases
the local database completely on both Android and iOS).

Exported backup files are your own responsibility once created. We have no
way to access, retrieve, or delete a file you've exported and shared or
stored elsewhere, since it never passes through us.

## 5. Your Choices and Rights

- **Export** your data as a backup file anytime, from Settings.
- **Import** a previous backup file.
- **Delete** individual entries, or all data, from within the app.
- **Limit ad personalization** via your device's ad settings (Android) or
  by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt (iOS) — you'll still
  see ads, just less targeted ones.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as
access, correction, deletion, or objection) over the advertising data
described in §1.2. Because that data is collected and held by Google, not
us, those requests are best directed to Google directly — see
https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://adssettings.google.com.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Period Tracker is not directed at, marketed to, or knowingly used by
children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under
13 (or a higher age where local law requires it). If you believe a child
has provided information through the app's advertising SDK, contact us
at the address below and we'll take reasonable steps to address it — we
hold no cycle data or personal information of our own to delete, since we
never receive it.

## 7. Sensitive Health Information

Menstrual cycle information can be treated as sensitive personal or health
information under the laws of some places. The app is built around that:
your cycle data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.
If you choose to export, share, or back up that file to cloud storage or
anywhere else, its privacy and security become your responsibility from
that point on — treat it the way you'd treat any other personal health
record.

## 8. No Medical Advice

Period Tracker provides estimates and predictions based on the dates you
log. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice,
diagnosis, or treatment. Predictions can be wrong, especially with
irregular cycles or a short logging history. Always consult a qualified
healthcare professional for medical advice, contraception, or fertility
planning — don't rely on this app for either.
