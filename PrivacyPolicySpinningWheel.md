# Privacy Policy — Wheel

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Wheel application ("Wheel," "the App") handles information when you use it on iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets. Wheel is developed by OSDev ("we," "us," "our").

## Summary

Wheel is designed to work entirely on your device. Your wheels, slices, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone, iPad, phone, or tablet and are never sent to us or to any server we control. We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information to use the App. The only outside data flow in the App comes from the third-party advertising service (Google AdMob) used to show ads, described below.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally, on your device only:

- The wheels and slices you create (names, text, colors, weights, order).
- Your app preferences (theme, accent color, spin time, remove-once-chosen setting, tap-to-stop setting, selected language).

This data is not transmitted to OSDev, and we have no access to it. If you delete the App, this data is deleted along with it, unless you have separately exported it (see below).

## Import / Export

The App includes an optional feature to export your wheels to a file and import them back in. This file is created and stored using your device's own share and file tools (for example, saving it to the Files app, or sending it via another app you choose), and importing uses the system file picker. We do not receive a copy of this file, and this feature does not involve any OSDev server.

## Advertising

The App displays advertisements served through **Google AdMob**: a banner ad on the Home screen, and an occasional full-screen (interstitial) ad shown at natural points such as after creating a new wheel. To serve and measure ads and to keep them secure, the Google Mobile Ads SDK that is built into the App may collect and process:

- your device's advertising identifier (the Advertising Identifier, "IDFA," on iPhone and iPad; the Advertising ID on Android) — on iPhone and iPad only if you allow it, see below;
- general device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, app version, language);
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- how you interact with ads (for example impressions and taps); and
- diagnostic information such as crash reports and performance data.

This processing is carried out by Google and governed by Google's own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: `https://policies.google.com/privacy`
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: `https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites`

We do not control, and are not responsible for, how Google or its advertising partners handle this information beyond what is described in their own policies. Information collected through the SDK is not combined by us with any information about you, because we hold none.

## Your Advertising Choices

**iPhone and iPad.** The first time you open the App, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether Wheel may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose **Ask App Not to Track**, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized; the App works exactly the same. You can change this at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

**Android.** You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (typically **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, or through your Google account's ad settings).

Wheel works fully whether or not you allow personalized ads.

## Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and the App does not require:

- Your name, email address, or phone number
- Account registration or login of any kind
- Access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location (beyond the approximate, IP-based location used by the advertising SDK above)

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your wheels and settings stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete individual wheels, or everything, at any time from within the App (**Settings → Delete All Data**), or by uninstalling the App. Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

## Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, biometrics, device encryption). Please keep exported backup files somewhere you trust.

## International Data Transfers

Google may process advertising-related information in the United States and other countries where Google or its service providers operate. We do not transfer your wheels or settings anywhere.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, or to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell personal information, and because we do not collect or store personal information ourselves, we generally have nothing to access, correct, or delete. For information held by Google, you can use Google's privacy controls at `https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy`. You can contact us with any request using the details below and we will help where we can.

## Children's Privacy

Wheel is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App itself stores data only locally and does not collect personal information, its own functionality does not involve children's personal data; however, the third-party advertising SDK described above is a general-audience service. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App in a way that concerns you, please contact us using the details below.
