# Privacy Policy for Days Until

**Last updated:** October 31, 2026

## Overview
Days Until ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Days Until collects, how it's used, and your choices — for both the app and this policy itself. Days Until is published by OSDev (France), which is responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

## Information We Collect
Days Until works on your device and does not need an account. Here is what applies:

- **Usage and device information (analytics)**: Days Until uses **Firebase Analytics**, a Google service, to understand how the app is used so we can improve it. Firebase Analytics records events it collects automatically, such as installs, app opens, sessions and app updates, together with technical information: an app-instance identifier, the app version, your device model, operating system version and language, and your approximate location (country or city, derived from your IP address, which Google does not store as part of this data). On iOS the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking. Analytics never includes the content of your countdowns or your photos. Where the law requires your consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), analytics stays off until you accept in the consent message, and it is switched off again if you withdraw your consent.
- **Advertising information**: The advertising service we use, **Google AdMob**, which shows banner ads and app open ads, may collect device identifiers (the Android Advertising ID or, on iOS, Apple's advertising identifier if you allow tracking), your IP address (from which it can infer an approximate location), device model, operating system version, and information about the ads shown and how you interact with them. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive or store this information ourselves.
- **Notifications from us**: Days Until uses **Firebase Cloud Messaging**, a Google service, so that we can send you notifications, for example news about new features. For this, Firebase creates an installation identifier and a registration token for the app on your device. We do not receive your name, email address or location through them. Notifications are shown only if you allow notifications for Days Until in your device settings, and you can turn them off there at any time.
- **Crash reports**: We do not use a crash-reporting service. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with Google or Apple in your device settings, those companies may provide us with aggregated, anonymous performance statistics.
- **Location**: Days Until does not request or use your device's location. The approximate location mentioned above is derived by Google from your IP address.
- **User content**: The countdowns you create (titles, dates, times, repeat and reminder settings, and the design, colors and font you choose) and the photos you pick as widget backgrounds are stored **only on your device**. We never receive them. When you pick a photo, Days Until saves a resized copy inside the app and removes its location and other metadata. If you use Share or Export, your content goes only to the app or location you choose. Your phone's backup service (for example Google or iCloud backup) may include Days Until's data, depending on your device settings.
- **Reminders**: Reminders you set for a countdown are scheduled locally on your device and do not need a server. They are separate from the notifications described above.
- **Account information**: Days Until does not require an account and stores no personal account information.

## How We Use Information
Content you create is used only on your device, to show your countdowns, widgets and reminders. Analytics data collected through Firebase Analytics is used to understand how the app is used (for example how many people use it and on which versions of Android or iOS) and to find and fix problems. Firebase Cloud Messaging is used only to deliver notifications to you. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is used by Google to deliver, measure and (where you allow it) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. We do not use any information for other purposes and we have no access to the content you create.

## Third-Party Services
Days Until uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:
- **Google AdMob** (advertising), including Google's **User Messaging Platform** (consent messages) — Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (analytics and notifications), provided by Google — Privacy and security in Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Data Sharing & Disclosure
We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the third-party services listed above, as required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Days Until, our users, or the public.

## Data Retention
Everything you create in Days Until stays on your device until you delete it (Settings → Delete all data) or uninstall the app. Backups you export are files you control. We run no servers of our own that hold information about you. Analytics event data held by Google through Firebase is kept for a limited period that we configure (no longer than 14 months). The identifiers used for notifications last until you uninstall the app or clear its data, or until Google expires them after a long period of inactivity. Advertising data held by Google is kept according to Google's own retention practices, described in the Google Privacy Policy linked above. Device identifiers last until you reset or delete them in your device settings.

## Your Rights & Choices
Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or state privacy laws in the United States), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your information, to withdraw consent, to receive a copy of your data, and to opt out of personalized advertising. To exercise these rights, contact us at omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com. Because analytics and advertising data is not linked to your name or email address, we usually cannot find it on our own; most requests are handled through Google, which you can reach through your Google account's privacy controls, or by uninstalling the app, which ends the collection tied to that installation. If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you can complain to your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Your choices:
- **Consent for ads and analytics:** In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Days Until shows a consent message before ads are requested and before analytics starts. Where it applies, you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy Options**. Where the GDPR applies, this data is processed by Google on the basis of your consent.
- **Notifications:** You decide whether Days Until may show notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your device's notification settings for the app.
- **iOS tracking:** Days Until asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are not personalized using your activity across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- **Android advertising ID:** You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google settings under Ads.
- **Your content:** You can delete any countdown from its Preview screen, or everything at once with Settings → Delete all data.

## Children's Privacy
Days Until is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app may be used by a mixed-age audience; we do not ask for your age, and ads shown in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience. In the EU, the age at which you can consent to the processing of your data online varies by country (for example 15 in France); below that age, a parent or guardian should decide whether to use the app. If we learn we've collected personal information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly. A parent or guardian who believes their child has provided us with personal information can contact us at omransoliman.osdevapps@gmail.com to request its deletion.

## Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. Your countdowns and photos are stored in the app's private storage on your device; we recommend protecting your device with a screen lock.

## International Data Transfers
Days Until is developed in France. We do not operate servers of our own that hold your information. Google, which provides the advertising, analytics and notification services, may process data in countries outside your own, including outside the EU and EEA, under its own safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy and in Firebase's privacy documentation. If you're located outside France/the EU, information handled by our service providers may be transferred to and processed in France/the EU or other countries where those providers operate.
