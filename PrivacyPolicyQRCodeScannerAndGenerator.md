## 1. Overview

QR Code ("the app") is a QR code scanner and generator. This policy explains what information the app accesses, what it stores, and what — if anything — ever leaves your device.

## 2. Information We Collect

**Content you create or scan.** Every QR code you scan or create is stored locally on your device: its decoded/encoded content, its type, an optional label you give it, and a timestamp. This never leaves your device unless you choose to Share, Save, or Export it yourself.

**Camera.** Used only to detect and decode QR codes in real time while you're actively scanning. The app does not record or store camera footage, and does not transmit anything the camera sees.

**Photos.** Used only when you choose to (a) pick an existing photo to scan a QR code from it, (b) pick a photo to encode into a QR code you're creating, or (c) save a generated QR code image to your photo library. The app does not browse or access your photo library beyond the specific photo you pick, and does not access your library at all unless you initiate one of these actions.

**No account.** The app doesn't require sign-up or a login, and doesn't collect your name, email, or any personal profile information itself.

## 3. How We Use Information

Solely to run the app's core functionality: storing your scan and creation history on-device, and displaying it back to you. Export and Import are entirely user-initiated — your data leaves the device, or gets read back in, only when you explicitly choose to do so.

## 4. Data Storage & Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device. It's removed when you delete an individual item, use "Delete All Data" in Settings, or uninstall the app. We do not operate a server that stores your scanned or created codes.

## 5. Advertising

The app may display banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not share your scanned or created QR content with AdMob or any advertiser — advertising identifiers are handled entirely by Google's SDK, separately from the app's own local data.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share the content of your scanned or created QR codes with any third party. The only third-party service involved is Google AdMob (§5), which operates independently of the app's local data.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Your Choices

- Delete any individual item at any time from its detail screen.
- Delete everything at once from Settings → Delete All Data.
- Export your data at any time from Settings → Export.
- Camera and Photos permissions can be revoked at any time in your device's system settings; doing so limits the corresponding features (scanning, scan-from-photo, save-to-photos) but doesn't affect anything already stored on-device.
